What these prices assume

Qloo's API is free for this hackathon. A commercial launch needs a usage agreement with Qloo, whose pricing is not public. Our plans leave room for that cost and for card fees, but the final numbers depend on that agreement. We would rather say so than show prices that only work on a free key.

Questions

Why a one-off option?

Most makers need this once, when they launch or relaunch. Asking them to subscribe for a single report would cost us most of them. The subscriptions are for people who advertise every month and need the taste data and ads kept fresh.

What counts as a report?

One product link, analysed as a single page or as a whole website, with its ad kit. Opening, sharing or re-reading a report is free.

What does the monthly refresh do?

Taste moves. Once a month we re-run the taste agents and the ad kit for every product you track, so the references and city variants stay current.

Why is it free now?

Who Would Love is a demo built for the Qloo Agentic Hackathon (2026). Until judging ends, reports are free for judges, and open to everyone from November 1 with a small daily limit.
